How to Find the Resources Tab
In the Solution Explorer (left side), find the project node named Project_Names(the one with the green C# icon).

Right-click that node.

Select Properties at the very bottom of the menu.

A new large tab will open in the middle of your screen. On the left side of this new tab, you will see a list of categories—click on Resources.

If it says the project does not contain a default resources file, click the link to create one.

Change the dropdown at the top from "Strings" to Images.

Drag and drop your PNG logo into this window.
